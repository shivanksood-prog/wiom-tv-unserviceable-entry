Repeat user — a way back to Wiom TV

Someone tries Wiom TV from the unserviceable screen, quits, and reopens the app days later. They land on "check my area" — a screen about a product they already know they can't have, with no route back to the one they can. This gives them one.

The job

What we're trying to do

  • Recover a user we already earned once. They came back to the app — but the app has nothing for them.
  • Make the return one tap, and resume on the channel they were watching.
  • Keep the booking funnel completely intact — this is an addition for a specific audience, never a replacement.

Design constraints we set

  • Small. A fifth of the screen width — this is secondary to booking, and must look it.
  • No overlap. It sits in the layout flow 5dp above the text below it, so it cannot cover anything.
  • Not a second pink CTA. The booking button keeps the screen's only solid brand-600 block.

Who actually sees it

Unserviceable user is told Wiom Net isn't available
→
Taps the band wiom.tv opens · flag latches
→
Later launch lands on "check my area"
→
Card appears 0.6s after render

Both conditions must hold: the Remote Config switch is on and this device has opened Wiom TV before. A first-time visitor sees today's landing screen byte-for-byte. Note the landing screen itself only shows for users without a session, so existing Wiom customers never see this.

Open for Product / Design

  • The flag latches on tapping the band, not on seeing it — users who hit unserviceable and didn't tap are excluded. Should they be included? They've also been told we can't serve them.
  • Other Wiom TV entry points (payment-success banner) don't latch the flag, so those users aren't recognised as returning. Each needs the same one-liner.
  • The card currently has no dismiss. Should it collapse to an edge tab after a few seconds, or persist?
  • It appears on every launch for a qualifying user — cap it after N impressions without a tap?
