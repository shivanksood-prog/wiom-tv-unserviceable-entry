Unserviceable screen — offer the product they can actually have

Today, when someone checks their area and Wiom Net isn't available, we say no and the journey ends. This adds one thing to that screen: Wiom TV, playing live, free, on the phone already in their hand.

The job

What we're trying to do

  • Turn a dead end into an offer — the user leaves with something they can use today, not just a rejection.
  • Prove the demand signal for Wiom TV with an audience that is already in our funnel and already disappointed.
  • Do it without touching the booking flow — the location CTA and login link are exactly as they are today.

Why a live picture, not a banner

  • "Free live TV" as text is a claim; a channel actually playing is proof.
  • Our users are on 2–4GB Android phones — they recognise the channel bug and the programme instantly.
  • The window is the single most expensive element on the screen, and it earns it: it is the pitch.

Tech

AreaDetail
Wherefeature/booking/ui/WiomTvPreviewBand.kt (new, self-gating) mounted from UnserviceableScreen.kt. Pre-booking variant only (fromConfirmLocation = false); the post-booking install-confirm screen is untouched.
GateWIOM_TV_BAND_ENABLED — Remote Config, default false. The composable renders nothing when off, so the screen mounts it unconditionally and the kill switch needs no release.
ChannelWIOM_TV_CHANNEL_NUM / WIOM_TV_CHANNEL_URL — one source of truth for what plays here and what wiom.tv opens via ?ch=. Default Dangal TV, chosen on 14 days of live stability data (start-rate and stall-rate per channel). Two fallbacks if the primary fails.
TimingWIOM_TV_BAND_ENTRY_DELAY_MS (1200) and WIOM_TV_PREVIEW_SECONDS (60), both RC-tunable; 0 = immediate / never stop.
PlayerExoPlayer (media3, already in the project) + new media3-exoplayer-hls for .m3u8. Muted, no controls, pauses on background, resumes on return, released in DisposableEffect.
Data guardThese users have no Wiom net — the stream costs them cellular. Playback stops at the budget and freezes on the last frame; no replay affordance, because a preview that played and paused should still read as live TV.
Analyticswiom_tv_band_shown (fires when it actually appears, not on mount) and wiom_tv_band_clicked with the channel. Outbound link carries ?ch=&src=app_unserviceable&ctid= so wiom.tv traffic is attributable to this screen.
LayoutThe band is tall, and on a 360×640dp phone the screen cannot hold window + address + title + CTAs + illustration. Resolution: message and address moved into the action zone (a Scaffold measures its bottom bar before its content, so they are structurally guaranteed), and the illustration is dropped on this variant. The QA-only skip control rides the login row instead of taking one of its own.
VerificationChecked on a headless emulator at 360×640dp by dumping the view hierarchy and asserting node bounds — not by eyeballing screenshots. ViewModel unit tests cover the gate, the deep link and the events.

⚠ Found on the way — affects the whole app, not this screen

WiomTextStyle.T3_20_* packs 20sp text into a 28sp line box (1.4×, where the body styles use 1.5×). Compose trims glyphs to that box and Devanagari's shirorekha sits above the Latin ascent — so the top stroke of every letter is sliced off on any 20sp Hindi heading in the app. We're a Hindi-default app, so this is likely visible in many places. Fixed locally in this PR; the app-wide fix is one line in Type.kt and needs a design-system decision.
