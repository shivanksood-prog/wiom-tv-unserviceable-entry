PRD · Wiom TV entry points in the customer app

Meet the user where we already said no

Two surfaces in the customer app hand a user to Wiom TV, and one contract that carries which channel, where they came from and who they are across the handoff. This document covers the product requirements and the technical contract; the design detail for each surface lives on its own page.

1 · Problem

The customer app has exactly one job today: sell Wiom Net. When it can't — because the user's area isn't served — the journey ends with an apology. That's a large, qualified, disappointed audience walking away with nothing, on a day when we have a second product that is free, live right now, and needs no installation.

And once a user does try Wiom TV, the app forgets. They reopen it days later, land on "check my area", and there is no route back to the thing they actually used.

2 · Goals & non-goals

Goals

  • Give an unserviceable user something usable the same minute, instead of a dead end.
  • Give a returning Wiom TV user a one-tap route back, resuming on the same channel.
  • Make wiom.tv traffic from the app attributable per surface, so we can tell first contact from re-entry.
  • Ship behind switches so the whole thing can be turned on, tuned or pulled without an app release.

Non-goals

  • Changing the booking funnel — the location CTA, login link and serviceability logic are untouched.
  • Building a player inside the app. We hand off to wiom.tv; the app only previews.
  • Account linking or login on wiom.tv. The handoff carries an analytics identity, not an authenticated session.
  • Monetisation. Wiom TV here is free-to-air, and the copy says so.

4 · Functional requirements

FR-1 · S1 visibility

The band renders on the unserviceable screen only in the pre-booking variant (fromConfirmLocation = false) and only when WIOM_TV_BAND_ENABLED is true. The post-booking install-confirm variant is out of scope for v1.

FR-2 · S1 sequencing

The screen's own message, address and primary CTA must render first. The band enters after WIOM_TV_BAND_ENTRY_DELAY_MS (default 1200) by sliding up from the bottom. It must never displace or overlay the message, the address or the booking CTA.

FR-3 · S2 visibility

The re-entry card renders on the landing screen only when WIOM_TV_REENTRY_ENABLED is true AND the device has previously opened Wiom TV (hasVisitedWiomTv). A user who has never seen Wiom TV must get today's landing screen byte-for-byte.

FR-4 · Preview behaviour (both)

A real channel plays muted, with no player controls. Playback stops after the configured budget (S1 60s, S2 30s) and freezes on the last rendered frame — no replay affordance. The player pauses when the app backgrounds, resumes on return, and is released when the composable leaves.

FR-5 · Never a black box

A poster is shown until the first frame renders and is retired permanently once the stream has played, so a frozen or paused preview can never look broken. If the primary channel fails, the player walks a fallback chain before giving up.

FR-6 · Handoff

Tapping anywhere on either surface opens wiom.tv with the contract in §5. The channel opened must be the channel that was playing in the preview.

FR-7 · Identity latch

Opening Wiom TV from the band sets a durable device flag (hasVisitedWiomTv). This is the sole qualifier for S2 today. DECISION NEEDED — see §10.

5 · The handoff contract — app → wiom.tv

This is the part that spans two codebases and two teams, so it is specified rather than implied. The app is the producer; wiom.tv is the consumer.

⚠ Required change on wiom.tv — this is the blocker

Verified in public/wiom-tv.html on origin/main: wiom.tv serialises every landing query param into entry_params, so src and ctid already flow into analytics. But nothing reads ch= — there is no code path that lands a user on a specified channel. Until that exists, a user who taps a live Dangal TV preview arrives on whatever the default landing channel is. The preview promise and the landing experience don't match.

Ask: in the boot path, read ch from location.search, resolve it against the channel list, and tune to it instead of the default — with a silent fallback when the channel is unknown, off-air or health-gated out.

Sequence

App RC gives channel num + URL
→
Preview plays that channel muted
→
Tap builds URL with ch + src + ctid
→
Browser opens wiom.tv
→
wiom.tv logs entry_params, tunes to ch

Failure modes

  • No browser available — the app wraps the intent in runCatching; a failed launch is silent, never a crash.
  • Channel unknown to wiom.tv — must degrade to default landing, not an error.
  • RC unavailable in the app — defaults apply: surfaces stay hidden, so no broken handoff is possible.
  • Blank analytics id — ctid is dropped from the URL rather than sent empty.

Privacy

ctid is a CleverTap device identifier, not a phone number, name or address. It travels in a URL, so it is visible in browser history and any referrer — acceptable for an analytics id, and deliberately not the phone number. Note wiom.tv already receives ?phone= on some other entry paths; this handoff does not add to that.

All keys live in the existing customer_kotlin_app_config blob. A missing or corrupt blob falls back to the defaults above — which keep both surfaces off. Failure mode is exclusion, never a half-configured surface.

Success metrics. Primary: tap-through rate per surface (_clicked ÷ _shown). Secondary: median watch-minutes of app-referred sessions vs organic, and D1 return of the app-referred cohort. Guardrail: no regression in the booking funnel — "choose different location" and login tap rates on the unserviceable screen must not move.

Analytical caveat worth deciding before we read results

wiom.tv runs a live landing experiment (fse_v2, four arms). App-referred users arrive with intent and a specified channel, so they behave nothing like organic first-time visitors — and as new uids they would be enrolled into that experiment and dilute it. Recommend excluding src=app_* sessions from fse_v2 enrolment, and reading them as their own cohort.

8 · Non-functional

  • Data. These users have no Wiom net — a muted stream burns their cellular. Bounded by the preview budget, stopped on background.
  • Devices. Target is 2–4GB Android phones. One ExoPlayer instance per surface, released on dispose; no player is retained across screens.
  • Layout. Verified at 360×640dp, the smallest realistic profile. The message and address are pinned in the action zone so they cannot be squeezed by the band.
  • Dependency. One addition: androidx.media3:media3-exoplayer-hls for .m3u8. media3 core and ui were already present.

9 · Rollout

1 Merge with both switches off — production unchanged
→
2 wiom.tv ships ?ch= support
→
3 Enable S1, watch tap-through + booking guardrails
→
4 Enable S2 once S1 has produced a returning cohort

S2 is deliberately second: it has no audience until S1 has created one, since the qualifying flag is only set by tapping the band. Kill switch is either flag flipped to false — effective on the next config fetch, no release.
