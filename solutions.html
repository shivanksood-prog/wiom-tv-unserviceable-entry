Wiom TV re-entry · landing screen only

Bring the user back to Wiom TV — one addition, primary flow untouched

A user tries Wiom TV on the unserviceable screen, quits, and relaunches onto “check my area” with no way back. We add one secondary re-entry on that landing screen — and we do not touch the booking / serviceability code, even where changing it would help.

✓ What we change

  • Who sees a Wiom TV re-entry on the landing screen
  • A single, clearly-secondary element — added, never replacing

What we don’t touch

  • The booking / “check my area” funnel — hero & CTA unchanged
  • Serviceability logic, routing, navigation history
  • No new backend, no new dependency

⏸ Parked on purpose — re-check ideas

Auto re-checking the area on return (location error, address change, coverage expansion) would strengthen objective 1 — but that flow isn’t ours to change right now. We stick to the Wiom TV problem and leave the primary utility exactly as it is. Noted as a future option for whoever owns booking.

Tech · the only decision is who sees the re-entry

Every option is purely additive to the landing screen — a conditional widget. None reads or writes booking/serviceability code. Leanest first.

W1

Everyone

0 backend~10 LOCno primary touch

A permanent secondary Wiom TV re-entry on the landing screen for all users. No flag, no state — just an always-present element.

Leanest, but always-on for people who never opened Wiom TV.

✓ Recommended W2

Returning Wiom TV users only

0 backend~20 LOCno primary touch

Set a local flag in shared_preferences when the user sees/taps the band on the unserviceable screen. The landing reads it and shows the re-entry only to them. Clear it after a successful booking or an expiry.

Solves the exact problem, shows it only to people who care.

W3

Remote-Config gated

managed config~40 LOCno primary touch

Firebase RC (already wired) controls on/off, % rollout and audience (all vs flagged), layered on W2.

Kill / tune / stage without a release — the train is dormant.

Ship: W2 (flagged returners) + W3’s RC switch as a cheap kill/rollout control.
