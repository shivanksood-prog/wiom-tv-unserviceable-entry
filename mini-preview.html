Wiom TV re-entry · V4

Mini side preview — small, right-docked, nothing underneath it

V3’s floating pill spans most of the width and sits on top of the screen. V4 shrinks it to a small branded thumbnail, right-aligned and sitting 5px above the text below it — in the layout flow, so it can never cover the hero, the address, or the CTA. It also does the one thing a pill can’t: it shows live TV playing, which is the actual reason to come back.

What makes it different from V3

  • ~104×59dp instead of a full-width pill — about a fifth of the screen width
  • In the flow, 5px above the text — reserves its own height, cannot overlap
  • Branded and live — Wiom mark + व्योम TV wordmark + फ्री tag over a muted live stream
  • Collapses to an edge tab after 8s, so it’s never permanent furniture

What still doesn’t change

  • The booking hero, copy and primary CTA
  • Serviceability logic, routing, navigation history
  • Bottom action zone stays 100% booking

How “no overlap” is actually guaranteed

Floating usually means “painted on top and hope nothing important is under it”. Here the layout reserves the space, so the guarantee is structural rather than a judgement call about spacing.

Layout contract

  • The preview is a right-aligned Row in the content column with Modifier.padding(bottom = 5.dp) — it occupies real layout space directly above the next text, so overlap is impossible by construction rather than by careful spacing.
  • Nothing needs reserved padding any more: the flow itself reserves the height. The floating/absolute version stays available only for the collapsed tab (V4c), which sits in the screen’s dead right edge.
  • Hero graphic keeps weight(1f, fill = false), so the reserved space comes out of the graphic — never out of the title or the address.
  • Minimum tap target 48dp is met by the card at every size; the small variant pads its touch area beyond its visual bounds.
  • Collapse to the tab after 8s (AnimatedVisibility + slide to x = width - 34.dp), and drop the reserved padding with it.

Recommendation

V4a at 104×59dp, auto-collapsing to V4c after 8s. It is the only re-entry that answers “why would I go back?” with a picture instead of a promise, and sitting in the flow means it can ship without re-litigating the booking layout. Keep it Remote-Config gated and returning-users-only — the same flag the band already uses — so it can be pulled without a release.
