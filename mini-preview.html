B1 · Solid brand bar

Full-width brand-600 base. The loudest read of the six — unmistakably a Wiom product, at the cost of 16dp of picture and a second strong pink shape sitting just above the pink CTA.

What to judge it on

  • At arm’s length — does it read as live TV, or as an ad banner?
  • Against the CTA — the booking button is already brand-600. Two strong pinks on one screen is exactly what B2, B3 and B4 avoid.
  • At 88×50 — the wordmark must survive the smallest size; B5 and B6 drop the फ्री tag there.
  • Build cost is identical across all six — a Column with a bar, or a Box overlay. Pick on looks, not effort.

If you want my pick

B2 — dark bar with the pink rule. The screen already spends its brand-600 on the booking CTA; another solid pink block 60dp above it splits the eye, which is probably why B1 felt off. A neutral-900 chin with a 1.5dp brand hairline still says Wiom TV at a glance, keeps the picture the loudest thing in the card, and echoes the dark band already shipping on the unserviceable screen — so both Wiom TV surfaces read as one product. Take B3 if you want maximum picture, B6 only if we were positioning Wiom TV as premium, which we aren’t — it’s free-to-air.
